About

Background

I like being in the room where a product takes shape. Asking the hard questions early, pressure-testing assumptions, and turning an idea into a plan a team can confidently build is the work I enjoy most.

I did that across many industries as a Forward Deployed Engineer at Benmore Technologies, and I do it today as Lead AI Strategist at Prime Care Medical Group, partnering with the CTO on which projects the company takes on and how. The role has given me responsibility well beyond engineering, from negotiating with vendors and signing agreements to deciding who joins the team.

Building that team is one of the hardest and most important parts of the job. Every gap in skills eventually surfaces in the product, as slower design work or as risk in security and testing, so I've learned to build teams that cover the whole picture.

I studied Computer Science and Economics at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, which let me combine my love of math, data, and logic puzzles while building a strong foundation in software. Next, I want to keep building in high-impact environments, sharpening how teams work and helping shape the organizations I'm part of. In my spare time, I'm building a local venue discovery app in Chicago. Away from the screen: hockey, traveling somewhere warm, playing pool, and music. Let's connect and build something together.
